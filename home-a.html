Scope

What's included, and what isn't

The second column matters more than the first. It is cheaper to find out now than on the morning of the event.

Included

  • Delivery to your venue. We bring the floor to you.
  • Installation, in one placement. The floor is set once, in a spot agreed in advance.
  • Black, white, and wood panels in any combination. The arrangement is confirmed with your quote.
  • A custom centre graphic, if you want one. 3 × 4, 5 × 6, or 6 × 6 ft.
  • Insurance. We carry it. Ask and we will send the details to your venue.

Not included

  • Outdoor installations. We install indoors only.
  • Grass, gravel, or uneven ground. The floor needs a hard, level surface. We do not lay a subfloor.
  • Customer pickup. We deliver; the floor is not collected from us.
  • A second placement. The floor is not moved once it is down.
  • Travel beyond Kansas City as standard. We do go further — it is quoted case by case.

If you are not sure what your venue's floor is made of, that is a normal answer. Choose “I'm not sure” on the form and we will work it out with you.
