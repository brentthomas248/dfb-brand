Pick a colour. Pick a typeface. They are two separate decisions.

You said some of the five directions felt similar. You were right — four of the five sat on a light background, and all five used the same kind of type pairing. So the range has been widened on both axes and then pulled apart, so you can decide independently what you like about the colour, what you like about the lettering, and what you like about the overall attitude.

Eight palettes × eight typography systems = 64 combinations. Every one renders live below. Start from one of the five recommended pairings if you want a designed starting point, then change either side.

Three new palettes, chosen to break the sameness

Blue Hour sits in the mid-tone middle — neither a light page nor a dark one — and is the only cool option. Marquee uses one strong colour at real scale instead of a 2% accent. Panel removes colour entirely: black, white, and nothing else, because that is literally what you rent.

Eight typography systems, not one pattern

The original five all paired a display serif over a sans. These eight include an all-serif setting, a single-grotesque Swiss setting, an inverted sans-over-serif editorial setting, condensed event-signage capitals, and extended capitals that echo the shape of a floor.

Three things to know before you decide

Colour is not reliable on a screen

The same hex value looks warmer on one phone than on another, and Night Shift or True Tone will shift it again. Treat these as close, not exact. Anything you shortlist gets checked on a calibrated screen and in print before it is locked.

The fonts here are stand-ins

Nothing is downloaded, so every option is drawn with the nearest typeface already on your device. The real families are named in full for each option. The real thing will be sharper and better proportioned than what you see here — never worse.

This is a demonstration, not a layout

The fragment above exists to show colour and lettering doing real work at real sizes. It is not the site design. And no palette is final until it has been put next to your actual event photography — the photographs are the deciding vote.
